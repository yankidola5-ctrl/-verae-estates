About VERAÉ

Built by Nigerians, for Nigerians moving home.

We started VERAÉ because finding a verified home in Abuja, Lagos or Port Harcourt shouldn't mean months of agent runaround and undocumented land.

Our story

Eleven years of verified addresses

VERAÉ Estates was founded in Abuja with a simple frustration: too many Nigerians were losing money to fake listings, undocumented land and agents who disappeared after the deposit. We built a company where every title is checked before a property is ever listed.

Today we operate across Abuja, Lagos and Port Harcourt, working with buyers relocating within Nigeria, tenants who want a transparent lease, and diaspora investors who need someone they can trust to inspect a property on their behalf.

Our mission

Premium spaces, better living

To make finding, renting, buying and investing in Nigerian real estate as transparent and dignified as it should be — backed by verified documentation, honest pricing and advisors who answer the phone.

We measure ourselves by how few surprises a client has after signing, not by how fast we can close a deal.
